<p>The tray icon emits events for the following mouse events:</p>
<ul>
<li>click: triggered when the cursor receives a single left, right or middle click, including information on whether the mouse press was released or not</li>
<li>Double click: triggered when the cursor receives a double left, right or middle click</li>
<li>Enter: triggered when the cursor enters the tray icon area</li>
<li>Move: triggered when the cursor moves around the tray icon area</li>
<li>Leave: triggered when the cursor leaves the tray icon area</li>
</ul>
